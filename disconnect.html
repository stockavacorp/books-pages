Disconnect

To stop Stockava Books from accessing QuickBooks:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online, open the Intuit app connections page, and disconnect "Stockava Books". This revokes the token at Intuit.
  2. On the Stockava computer, delete the locally stored access and refresh tokens. This removes our ability to reconnect without a new approval.
  3. Locally stored accounting data can be deleted on request or at the same time.

After step 1, the app can no longer read your data.
