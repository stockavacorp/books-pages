Privacy Policy

Stockava Books is an internal tool used only by Stockava for its own company books.

  • What we access: accounting data of Stockava's own QuickBooks Online company, through read-only access. We never write to QuickBooks.
  • Where it is kept: on Stockava's own computer. It is not uploaded to any public service.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone.
  • Tokens: the access token is stored locally and deleted when access is disconnected.
  • Deletion: see Disconnect for how to revoke access and remove stored tokens.
