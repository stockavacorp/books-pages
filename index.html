Stockava Books

Stockava Books is a private, internal tool used by Stockava to read its own QuickBooks Online accounting data for bookkeeping review. It is not offered to the public or to other companies.

Launch / Connect

The app is launched by Stockava staff on their own computer. To connect, an authorized Stockava user signs in to QuickBooks Online and approves read-only access for the company file. To reconnect, repeat the same approval.

To end access, see Disconnect.
